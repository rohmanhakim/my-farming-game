# Onboarding — The Short Start

> **Status:** Proposal — new document
> **Version:** 0.6
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-21
> **Purpose:** How a new player (ages 6+) learns the game. Owner direction (2026-11-19): **short** — the guided part should take about **5 minutes**, not 30. After that, the game teaches through gentle, one-time nudges while the player plays freely.

---

## 1. Principles

- **Do, don't read.** Every step is one action with one picture prompt; text is one short line (read aloud if enabled).
- **One new thing at a time**, and only when it is needed.
- **Never block play.** Every nudge can be ignored; nothing waits for the tutorial.
- **Teach the real loop first:** produce → order → money → invest (GDD §3 *First order*).
- **Hints follow Marlow's hint ladder** (characters.md §8): world feedback → pointing icon → question → plain instruction.

## 2. Guided start (≈ 5 minutes, Day 1 morning)

| # | Step | What the player does | Teaches | ≈ Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival | Walk from the cart to the front door; Ada waves | Move | 30 s |
| 2 | The garden | Pull 3 weeds by the beds | Interact / clear | 30 s |
| 3 | Plant | Use the seed bag on the 3 cleared beds (carrots) | Plant | 30 s |
| 4 | Water | Water the 3 beds; soil darkens | Water every day | 20 s |
| 5 | Meet Marlow | Walk to the seed shed; Marlow asks for 3 handfuls of wild berries | Orders | 40 s |
| 6 | Forage | Pick berries from bushes on the lane (marked with a sparkle) | Forage | 60 s |
| 7 | First order | Hand the berries to Marlow → coins pop into the wallet; the **order board** appears | Money | 30 s |
| 8 | Second order | Marlow pins "5 carrots" to the board and points at Kit's garden | Orders match what you grow | 20 s |
| 9 | Feed the animals | Back home, Ada shows the 5 hens and the cow: fill each trough slot with the feed scoop (the arrow points at the next empty slot) | Animals need food every day | 40 s |
| 10 | Free play | A goal icon shows "Feed lasts 7 days 🌾 — earn coins for more" | Saving for a goal | — |

**Total ≈ 5 minutes.** From here the player is free.

## 3. One-time nudges (Days 1–7, only when relevant)

| Trigger | Nudge |
|---|---|
| First evening | Ada: "It's getting late. Sleep at home to start a new day." (bed icon) |
| Morning after planting | Watering-can icon over dry beds |
| A crop turns yellow (2 dry days) | Marlow or Ada: "That one's thirsty! Water it today." |
| A crop is lost (3 dry days) | Small note: "Dry too long. Water every day." (once) |
| Carrots ready | Sparkle on the beds + the order card lights up |
| Feed running low (2 days left) | Ada: "We're nearly out of feed. Call the feed seller — your parents left the number by the phone. Before 6 in the evening!" (phone icon) |
| First morning | "Collect eggs and milk!" (icons over the coop and barn) |
| First eggs and milk | Egg and milk orders appear on the board |

*From Phase-002 (owner 2026-11-21):* steps 7–8 and the nudge above change with house-visit orders — Marlow's second order comes from a sign on his cottage door, the board becomes the village noticeboard, and the guided start should also show the shipping box and the Orders button. Rework this table when Phase-002 is planned.
| Hen unfed for 2 days | Ada: "The hens are hungry!" |
| First rain | "Rain waters the garden for you today." |

Each nudge shows once. A settings toggle turns all nudges off.

## 4. What is deliberately not taught early

Prices and demand, processing, buildings beyond the coop, clearing the back fields, automation — each gets its own one-line nudge the first time it becomes available (progression.md §3).

## 5. Validation

Playtest with 6–8-year-olds: can they finish the guided start without adult help, and do they water on Day 2 without being told twice?
